# Questionnaire 5

### Q1. The wheel's single category (club/league/nationality) — Round 4 said the wheel is never mixed. When is that one category actually chosen?

**Answer:** Once, at the very start of the draft — the whole draft uses that one category type throughout

### Q2. Round 4 said there are no constraints in Auction or Deal or No Deal. Does that mean the setting isn't offered at all, or does it exist but go unenforced?

**Answer:** Not offered at all. Actually, make constraints only for free pick.

### Q3. The squad-sharing “little image” per player (Round 4) — what should it actually be?

**Answer:** A small headshot/portrait of the footballer

### Q4. Who chooses Format / Scope / Constraints for a lobby's draft?

**Answer:** Only the lobby leader/host picks, before anyone else can act

### Q5. Are bots ever added to a lobby automatically, or only when a human explicitly adds one?

**Answer:** Always manual — a human (host) has to explicitly add each bot, same as inviting a player

### Q6. Auction — once a player's XI is already full, can they keep bidding to stock the graveyard, or are they done?

**Answer:** Yes — bidding has no connection to slot status; anyone can keep bidding for graveyard upgrades or to block others right up until the auction ends

### Q7. Deal or No Deal — turn order rotates so “a different player goes first” each round. How exactly does that rotation work?

**Answer:** Strict round robin — it just cycles through every player in a fixed order, one round each

### Q8. Does the graveyard show up anywhere in the finished squad-sharing image or the side-by-side comparison, or is it purely private?

**Answer:** Fully private — only the final XI ever appears in sharing or comparison

### Q9. For Free Pick / Spin the Wheel's constraint enforcement — are ineligible footballers hidden from you during your turn, or just blocked when you try to pick them?

**Answer:** Ineligible footballers are filtered out of what you can even select during your turn

### Q10. Lobby lifecycle — once a draft finishes, does the lobby stay open for another draft, or does it end?

**Answer:** The lobby stays open — the same group can immediately start a new draft with new settings
