How to read the graphs:
• Squad Score: The position-weighted quality of drafted 11-player squads (higher is better). Watch this rise as the bots learn player synergy.
• Challenger Win Rate: In self-play, new models compete against the reigning Champion. When win rate climbs above 55%, a new Champion is crowned. Around ~50%, the model is converging toward mastery.
• PPO Loss: Neural network regression loss per training epoch (decreases over time as predictions stabilize).
