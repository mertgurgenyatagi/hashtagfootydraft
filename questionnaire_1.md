# Questionnaire 1

### Q1. Running out of money (Auction & Deal or No Deal) — what happens when a player can't afford the pool's minimum starting bid anymore?

**Answer:** Your call

### Q2. Footballer pool selection per draft — how should the pool guarantee every position gets filled?

**Answer:** Your call

### Q3. Lobby size — how many drafters (humans + bots combined) should a lobby support?

**Answer:** 2 to 10. Not chosen at the start. Can't start without at least 2, can go up to 5. The number will be apparent once the lobby leader starts, no need to pick it at the start.

### Q4. Spin the Wheel — the wheel lands on a club/league/nationality with no eligible footballers left. What happens?

**Answer:** Your call

### Q5. Incomplete squads — can a draft finish with empty slots in the 4-2-3-1 still unfilled?

**Answer:** No, but it's up to you how to handle it.

### Q6. Turn / bid timers — should there be a time limit per turn or bid?

**Answer:** Your call

### Q7. Graveyard — should there be any limit or cost to how many footballers a player can stash there?

**Answer:** Unlimited — any purchased footballer can sit in the graveyard indefinitely

### Q8. Constraints — can multiple constraints run in the same draft (e.g. 1 per club AND 1 per nationality together)?

**Answer:** No — exactly one constraint is chosen per draft

### Q9. Bots — should players be able to set a bot's personality or aggressiveness before a draft starts?

**Answer:** No — bots always play one consistent default style

### Q10. Post-draft — once a squad is finalized, can it still be edited?

**Answer:** Formation/positioning of already-drafted footballers can be rearranged after, but the roster is locked
