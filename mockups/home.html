#footydraft — home screen, 20 layouts

Twenty different page-shapes, not twenty colour-swaps of one. Every frame carries the same job — pick a nickname, create a lobby, join with a code, or play solo — and every frame uses stadium.webp bleeding into its own background a different way. Five palette buttons under each frame. Click a frame to blow it up to full 1280×800, click again to shrink.
