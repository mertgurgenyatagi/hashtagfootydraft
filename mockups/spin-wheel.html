#footydraft — the Spin the Wheel draft screen, 20 layouts

Twenty page-shapes for one screen, not twenty dressings of one shape. Every frame does the same six jobs, all non-negotiable: put the wheel front and centre — never a corner widget — with its five slices carrying the real league colours, one step darker; spotlight the selected footballer the way the finalized Free Pick screen does, one photograph and nothing else on the card; show your eleven on a pitch, with tabs onto everyone else's; keep chat reachable; and use stadium.webp as visibly as the frame can carry it. The palette is petrol and does not change; type is Oswald and Inter. All the variety is structural. No motion — first look only.

Every player, club and crest is a real row of player_data.csv, scoped by club the way the app itself scopes — never the CSV's own League column. There are no ability ratings and no pool counts anywhere: data-model facts stay off screen. Spin the Wheel has no currency either, so the only numerals on these frames are the round and the pick count.

Click a frame to blow it up to full 1280×800; click again to shrink.
