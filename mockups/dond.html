#footydraft — the Deal or No Deal draft screen, 20 layouts

Twenty page-shapes for one screen, not twenty dressings of one shape. Every frame does the same six jobs: show the ten boxes and which are still shut, show the footballer who just came out of yours, put the banker's offer against it, make the decision unmissable, say whose go it is, and keep your eleven and chat reachable. The palette is petrol and does not change; type is Oswald and Inter. All the variety is structural. No motion — first look only.

This run is deliberately louder than the other three. The stadium plate sits at roughly double the opacity it carries on the Free Pick and Spin the Wheel screens and goes full-bleed on most frames; the decision type is set two or three steps larger than anything in the app today. That is the brief, not drift: this is the format where a box opens and somebody either takes the deal or gambles the round away, and it should look like it.

Every player, club, crest, nation and age is a real row of player_data.csv, scoped through src/data/clubs.ts the way the app scopes a draft. There are no ability ratings and no pool counts anywhere. Deal or No Deal has no currency at all — no budget exists in the rules — so the only numerals on these frames are the round, the clock and the box numbers.

Click a frame to blow it up to full 1280×800; click again to shrink.
