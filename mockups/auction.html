#footydraft — the Auction draft screen, 12 layouts

Twelve page-shapes for the one format still without a screen. Every frame satisfies all twelve non-negotiables set on 2026-08-21, so nothing here is picked by which requirement it dropped — only by how it arranges them. The centre stack is fixed in all twelve: the count of what is left sits at the top of the player displayer, the displayer sits above the countdown, and the countdown sits above the five drafters bidding side by side. Everything else moves.

Two things change here that are meant to go site-wide. Player photographs and club crests now carry their own colour, with no filter at all — the first screens in this app to do it. And the stadium plate runs at .55–.90 against the .30–.36 the earlier runs used. The plate stays monochrome and tinted on purpose: it is what makes the full-colour faces read as the only colour on the page. If it should go full colour too, say so — it is one line.

No sentences anywhere on any frame. Labels and values only. The queue is sealed — nothing previews an upcoming lot; sold lots are shown in full. Bidding is drawn as real time, not turn-based: no turn language, increments live for everyone at once, and the turn indicator is a holder readout tied to the clock. Type is Oswald + Inter, palette is petrol, no motion.
