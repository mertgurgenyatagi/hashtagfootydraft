#footydraft — the Free Pick draft screen, 20 layouts

Twenty page-shapes for one screen, not twenty dressings of one shape. Every frame does the same six jobs: show the pool you can pick from, show your eleven filling up, say whose turn it is and how long is left, show what the room has taken, keep the active constraint legible, and keep chat reachable. The palette is petrol and does not change; type is Oswald and Inter. All the variety is structural. No motion — first look only.

Every player, club, crest, nation and age is a real row of player_data.csv. There are no ability ratings and no pool counts anywhere on these frames: those are data-model facts and they stay off screen. Free Pick has no currency either — so the only numerals on a Free Pick screen are the clock, the round and the pick number. That turned out to be a useful constraint rather than a limitation: Auction is the numbers format, Free Pick is the names format, and these screens are quiet because of it.

Click a frame to blow it up to full 1280×800; click again to shrink.
