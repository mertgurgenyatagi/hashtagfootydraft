#footydraft — single-player lobby, 15 layouts

Resolved: layout 04 · Ready room was picked and is what gets built. The run stopped at fifteen of a planned twenty once that was settled — 16–20 were never coded, on purpose.

Fifteen page-shapes for one screen, not fifteen dressings of one shape. Every frame does the same job: choose a format, a scope, a constraint (Free Pick only — hidden or disabled everywhere else), how many bots sit at the table, a turn timer, and then kick off. The palette is petrol and it does not change; type is Oswald and Inter. All the variety is structural.

Every number on these frames is real, out of player_data.csv and PROJECT.md — 546 in the pool, the per-position counts, the opening bids, and the CM shortage that makes three of the five league scopes genuinely unusable. Nothing is invented. Click a frame to blow it up to full 1280×800, click again to shrink.
