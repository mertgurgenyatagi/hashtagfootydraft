# Questionnaire 4

### Q1. Constraint enforcement — can a player attempt to bid on or pick a footballer that would break their own constraint (e.g. they already own one from that club under “1 per club”)?

**Answer:** No constraints in the auction mode.

### Q2. Deal or No Deal constraint enforcement — the boxes are shared/identical for everyone in a round. If every remaining box that round would break one player's constraint, what happens to them?

**Answer:** No constraints in the doND mode.

### Q3. Disconnection — Round 3 said a bot takes over “after a long while”. How long, exactly?

**Answer:** 45 seconds

### Q4. Reconnection — if a disconnected player gets replaced by a bot mid-draft, can they take their seat back if they reconnect later in that same draft?

**Answer:** Yes — reconnecting always hands control back immediately

### Q5. Squad sharing image — Round 3 settled on a lineup graphic. What should it show per player?

**Answer:** Names, positions, little images. We're gonna have a lot of assets eventually.

### Q6. Spin the Wheel — should every category on the wheel have an equal chance, or should some be weighted?

**Answer:** Oh no. There are no cris crosses on the wheel. It's either all just clubs, all just leagues or all just nationalities.

### Q7. Lobby chat — does it stay fully active through fast-moving real-time moments like Auction bidding, or take a back seat?

**Answer:** Always fully active, no restrictions, regardless of format

### Q8. Lobby privacy — how does someone join a lobby?

**Answer:** Invite link only — no public lobby listing

### Q9. Spectators — can someone watch a draft without playing in it?

**Answer:** No — every participant in a lobby is a drafter, no spectator mode

### Q10. Footballer pool freshness — does every new draft start from the full player_data.csv pool again, or does it remember footballers used before?

**Answer:** Every draft starts completely fresh from the full pool, no memory of past drafts
