# Questionnaire 6

### Q1. Auction — footballers now reveal one at a time (Round 3). What order do they come up in?

**Answer:** Fully random order, no quality curve at all

### Q2. Deal or No Deal — Round 2 settled a general pool-wide skew toward higher-ability players. Does that skew apply to which footballers land in a given round's boxes, or are that round's boxes pulled evenly from everyone eligible for the position?

**Answer:** Yes — boxes follow the same higher-ability skew as the rest of the pool

### Q3. Constraints (Free Pick only) — “1 per club” or “1 per nationality”, counted against what exactly?

**Answer:** Per player's own squad only — you personally can't have two from the same club/nationality

### Q4. Free Pick — if a player's constraint filters out every remaining footballer eligible for a position they still need, but the Squad Completion Guarantee says they can't finish incomplete, what happens?

**Answer:** I don't think that will ever happen.

### Q5. Turns & Timers — what should the actual default timer length be (before a host changes it)?

**Answer:** Short — around 15 seconds

### Q6. Host transfer — if the lobby leader disconnects or leaves, does host status move to someone else?

**Answer:** Yes — it automatically passes to whoever joined the lobby next earliest

### Q7. Auction — Round 5 allowed bidding purely to block, even with a full XI. Could a well-funded player keep the whole auction running indefinitely just to deny others?

**Answer:** Yes, and that's fine — it's a legitimate, intended part of the strategy

### Q8. Deal or No Deal — the AI proposer's offer is deliberately a bit worse than the boxes' average (Round 3). Does that offer adjust to the specific player it's made to, or is it the same flat calculation for everyone?

**Answer:** Same flat calculation for whoever's offered it that round — position-based only, not player-specific

### Q9. Squad sharing image — is it generated automatically the moment a draft ends, or does a player trigger it manually whenever they want?

**Answer:** Manually triggered — a player clicks “export” whenever they want one

### Q10. Lobby lifecycle (Round 5: lobbies stay open for another draft) — does anything carry over between back-to-back drafts in the same lobby?

**Answer:** Nothing carries over — every new draft is a completely clean slate, same as a brand-new lobby
