### Q3. Auction — how should bids raise once the floor is set?

**Answer:** +1, +5, stuff like that

### Q4. The Auction reserve mechanic (Round 1, Q1) holds back enough budget to fill remaining slots. What price should it reserve per empty slot?

**Answer:** I don't like that implementation. When you don't have any money and the game has "run its course" (you gotta decide what that means), it should just give you shitty players

### Q5. Auction — who opens bidding on each footballer, and does that role rotate?

**Answer:** No designated opener — it's a free-for-all the moment a footballer appears

### Q6. Free Pick and Spin the Wheel are snake drafts — how is the very first pick order decided?

**Answer:** Random draw when the draft starts

**Answer:** Don't delve into the bot logic

### Q9. There's no scoring system — what should “compare squads” actually show players?

**Answer:** A pure side-by-side visual of both formations, no numbers at all

**Answer:** Skew toward higher-ability players so drafts feel more star-studded
