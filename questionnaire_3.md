# Questionnaire 3

### Q1. Auction — Round 2 dropped the idea of a designated opener, it's a free-for-all the moment a footballer appears. So what actually makes a footballer appear?

**Answer:** The system auto-reveals one footballer at a time, a new one appearing as soon as the current one sells or passes

### Q2. Spin the Wheel's graveyard — does it follow Free Pick's rule (none) or Auction/Deal or No Deal's (unlimited)?

**Answer:** No graveyard. Also, I don't think there's a graveyard in DoND. There shouldn't be, no. Because it's 11 rounds.

### Q3. Scope × Spin the Wheel — the briefing only covers Top 5 Leagues and one-league Scopes. What about the other two?

**Answer:** Your call

### Q4. Deal or No Deal — how should the AI proposer choose what to offer? (A format rule for every player, not bot personality.)

**Answer:** Deliberately a bit worse than that average, to make sticking with the boxes tempting

### Q5. Squad sharing — the briefing says squads “can be shared”. What should that produce?

**Answer:** A downloadable/shareable image — a lineup graphic — of the finished squad

### Q6. Disconnection — a player drops mid-draft. What happens?

**Answer:** After a long while, they are replaced by a bot.

### Q7. Single-player — when one human plays alone against bots, how many bots fill the rest of the lobby?

**Answer:** The human picks the bot count at setup, within the 2–5 range

### Q8. Multi-position footballers — one listed as “AMF, RW, LW” is eligible at three slots. Once drafted, how does that flexibility work?

**Answer:** One footballer, one slot, but freely reassignable among their listed eligible positions afterward

### Q9. Auction bid increments — Round 2 settled “flat, stepped” (+1, +5, +10 style). What should the actual steps look like?

**Answer:** A small fixed set of buttons — e.g. +1M / +5M / +10M — available at every price point

### Q10. “The auction has run its course” triggers the backfill. Should it be a hard stop for everyone, or per player?

**Answer:** Hard stop — once every remaining player can't afford anything more, the whole auction ends immediately for everyone
